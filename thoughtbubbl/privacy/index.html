Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026 · Last updated September 12, 2026

The short version: thoughtbubbl does not collect your personal data. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no servers of ours that your content is sent to. Everything you write in the app is stored on your device and stays there. We have no way to read it, and we have nothing to sell, share, or lose in a breach.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the thoughtbubbl mobile app (“thoughtbubbl,” “the app”) handles information. thoughtbubbl is made by RIVULINE Media (“we,” “us,” “our”).

It applies to the app itself and to this website at rivuline.com. It does not cover any third-party service you may separately choose to use, including the Apple App Store.

2. Information we collect

None. thoughtbubbl does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information about you on our systems. Specifically, the app does not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details
  • Account credentials — the app has no accounts and no sign-in
  • The content of anything you write, save, or create in the app
  • Your location, contacts, photos, calendar, or health data
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or IP addresses
  • Usage analytics, session recordings, or behavioral data

We do not operate a backend server for thoughtbubbl. There is no database anywhere holding your information, because the app never sends it to us.

3. Information stored on your device

The notes, thoughts, and settings you create in thoughtbubbl are saved locally in the app’s private storage area on your device. This data:

  • Stays on your device and is never transmitted to us
  • Is protected by your device’s own security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, and operating-system file protection)
  • Is not accessible to us, to other apps, or to anyone who does not have access to your unlocked device

If you have device backups turned on (for example, iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup), your device’s operating system may include thoughtbubbl’s local data in those backups. That backup is between you and your device maker — it is governed by their privacy policy, not ours, and we have no access to it.

4. Permissions the app requests

thoughtbubbl requests only the permissions it needs to function, and only at the moment a feature needs them. If you decline a permission, that feature simply does not run — the rest of the app keeps working. Any data reached through a permission you grant is used on your device for that feature alone and is never sent to us.

5. Third-party services and tracking

thoughtbubbl contains no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook/Meta SDKs, advertising networks, or any similar service inside the app.

The app does not track you across other apps or websites, and it does not ask for permission to do so under Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework, because there is nothing to track.

6. What Apple collects

When you download thoughtbubbl from the App Store, Apple handles that transaction and may collect information about the download as part of running its store. If you have chosen to share analytics and crash data with developers in your device settings, Apple may also provide us with aggregate, anonymized statistics — for example, crash reports or total download counts. This data does not identify you, and we cannot use it to contact you or to see what you have written in the app.

Apple’s collection is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, not this one. You can change what you share with developers in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

7. Sharing and selling of data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with anyone, because we do not have any of it. We do not disclose personal information for targeted advertising or for any cross-context behavioral advertising.

If we were ever compelled by valid legal process to produce user data, we would have nothing to produce: the data lives on your device, not with us.

8. Data retention and deletion

We retain nothing, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. Your thoughtbubbl data exists only on your device, and you are in full control of it:

  • Delete individual items from within the app
  • Clear all app data from within the app’s settings, where offered
  • Delete the app from your device — this removes its locally stored data along with it

If you have device backups enabled, a copy may persist in those backups until they are overwritten or removed through your device or cloud account settings.

9. Security

The strongest privacy protection is not collecting data in the first place, and that is the approach thoughtbubbl takes. Beyond that, your data is protected by the security of your own device: keep your operating system up to date, use a passcode or biometric lock, and avoid leaving your device unlocked and unattended.

No method of electronic storage is perfectly secure. We cannot guarantee the security of data held on a device we do not control.

10. Children’s privacy

thoughtbubbl is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 — or from anyone else. The app has no accounts, no messaging, no social features, and no advertising.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will look into it, although as described above we do not receive personal information from any user.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information — including the right to access it, correct it, delete it, port it, or opt out of its sale or sharing. These include rights under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA.

Because thoughtbubbl collects no personal information, there is no data of yours for us to access, correct, export, or delete, and none to opt out of selling or sharing. Your data is already entirely under your own control on your device. You are welcome to contact us with any question about this, and we will respond.

California notice

We do not collect personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA, we do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information. We have not done so in the preceding twelve months.

12. International users

RIVULINE Media is based in the United States. Because thoughtbubbl does not transmit your information anywhere, using the app does not involve any international transfer of your personal data.

13. Changes to this policy

If thoughtbubbl ever changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, if we add optional cloud sync or accounts — we will update this policy and change the “Last updated” date above before that change takes effect. For material changes, we will also provide notice within the app. Continuing to use thoughtbubbl after an update means you accept the revised policy.
