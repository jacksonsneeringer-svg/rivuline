Who we are

Small crew. Senior hands.

RIVULINE Media started with a simple frustration: great stories kept getting flattened by the process meant to deliver them. So we built a studio where the director is on set, the editor sat in the strategy call, and nothing gets lost between the pitch and the final cut.

We work with a handful of clients at a time — founders, marketing leads and agencies who want a partner rather than a vendor. That means honest timelines, one point of contact, and a finished piece that looks like what we promised.

How it works

Four steps, no surprises.

01

Conversation

A 30-minute call about the goal, the audience and the budget. You leave it knowing whether we’re the right fit — we’ll say so if we’re not.

02

Concept & quote

A written treatment, a shot approach and a fixed quote. One document, everything in it, nothing billed later that isn’t on the page.

03

Production

We handle crew, gear, locations and logistics. You show up, or you don’t — plenty of clients only see the footage at first cut.

04

Delivery

Two rounds of revisions, then every format you need: broadcast, web, vertical, stills. Source files stay yours, always.
